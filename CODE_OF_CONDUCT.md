# Code of conduct

This applies to every CARTIQO space: our repositories, issues and pull requests, our Discord, and anywhere someone is representing CARTIQO in public.

## The short version

Be someone people are glad to work with. Disagree about the work, not about the person doing it.

## What that looks like

- Assume the other person is trying to help until you have real evidence otherwise.
- Give feedback on the thing that was made. "This query will not scale past a few thousand rows" is useful. "This is amateur work" is not.
- Take feedback the same way. Somebody spending their time reviewing your code is doing you a favour, even when they are blunt about it.
- Accept that maintainers say no. A no on a feature is a decision about scope, not a judgement of you.
- Use plain language and be patient with people who are new, or who are working in their second or third language.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private messages.
- Insults or dismissals aimed at a person, and any attack based on age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity or orientation.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including their address or email, without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing threads, or repeatedly reopening a decision that has already been made.

## Reporting

Email [hello@cartiqo.xyz](mailto:hello@cartiqo.xyz). Tell us what happened, where, and when, and include links or screenshots if you have them.

Reports go to the studio, not to the wider community. We will not share your identity with the person you are reporting without asking you first. If your report is about someone in the studio, say so and it will be handled by someone else.

We would rather hear about something small early than something serious late.

## What we will do

We look into every report, and we get back to you. Depending on what we find, the response ranges from a private word, to a warning, to a temporary suspension, to a permanent ban from CARTIQO spaces. Enforcement decisions are ours to make and we make them in private, out of respect for everyone involved.

If you think we got a decision wrong, reply and say so. We would rather revisit a call than be quietly wrong about it.
