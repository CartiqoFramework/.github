# Security policy

We would much rather hear about a problem from you than from the people exploiting it.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Email [hello@cartiqo.xyz](mailto:hello@cartiqo.xyz)** with `SECURITY` in the subject, or use **Report a vulnerability** under the Security tab on the affected repository where private reporting is enabled.

Please do not open a public issue, post it in Discord, or tweet it before we have had a chance to fix it.

Useful things to include:

- What the issue is and roughly how bad you think it is.
- Steps to reproduce it, or a short proof of concept.
- The affected domain, repository or bot command.
- How you would like to be credited, if at all.

## What happens next

| When | What |
| :-- | :-- |
| Within 48 hours | We confirm we received it. |
| Within 7 days | We tell you whether we can reproduce it and what we intend to do. |
| Ongoing | We keep you updated while we work, and tell you when it is fixed. |

We are a small studio, not a security team with a rota, so those are honest targets rather than a contractual SLA. If you have not heard back in 48 hours, chase us.

## Scope

In scope:

- `cartiqo.xyz` and `cartiqo.app`, and their subdomains.
- Our Discord bots and their dashboards.
- Any public repository under [@CartiqoFramework](https://github.com/CartiqoFramework).

Out of scope:

- Anything hosted by a third party we merely use.
- Reports produced only by an automated scanner, with no demonstrated impact.
- Missing headers, missing SPF or DMARC records, and similar findings with no exploit path attached.
- Social engineering of our staff or our users, and denial of service testing.

## Ground rules

Test only against accounts, servers and data that are yours. Do not access, change or keep anyone else's data. Stop as soon as you have proved the issue, and give us a reasonable window to fix it before you publish.

Stay inside those lines and we will treat your report as good faith research and will not pursue action over it.

## Rewards

We do not run a paid bug bounty. We can offer credit in the fix, a thank you on this page, and our genuine gratitude.

## Impersonation and scams

Someone pretending to be CARTIQO is a security problem too. The accounts and domains that are actually ours are listed on our [profile](https://github.com/CartiqoFramework). We never ask for passwords, tokens or payment in a direct message. Report impersonation to [hello@cartiqo.xyz](mailto:hello@cartiqo.xyz) or with the [impersonation report template](https://github.com/CartiqoFramework/.github/issues/new?template=impersonation-report.yml).
